OCR and privacy

Text recognition uses Tesseract.js in your browser. PDF pages are rendered locally with PDF.js. Office documents are parsed on the encrypted account server. Printed English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam are available; handwriting recognition is best effort for English/Hindi. Verify every extracted value against the original. After account sign-in and your consent, account records are stored encrypted on the server. AI analysis sends extracted text to the configured provider only when you explicitly ask for it.

OCR may misread names, dates, units, decimal points, and handwriting. This tool does not diagnose or interpret results. Consult a qualified health professional.

Privacy & data

After sign-in, health records and original files are linked to your account and encrypted in the AYUSH+ server database. AI uses record text only for an analysis you request and confirm; it does not train or update its model from your records. Provider retention rules still apply.

Medical information noticeAYUSH+ is an organizational tool. AI-generated summaries are informational and are not diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for professional care.
